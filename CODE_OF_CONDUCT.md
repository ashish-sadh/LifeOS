# Code of Conduct

LifeOS is a personal tool that people use to build coaches for sensitive parts of their lives — health, relationships, productivity, creative work. That context shapes how we want this community to operate.

## The short version

Be direct, not unkind. Disagree on substance, not on people. Help each other ship better work.

## What this looks like

**In issues and PRs:**
- Critique the design or implementation, not the person who made it
- When something is unclear, ask before assuming bad intent
- If you're frustrated, take a minute before hitting reply
- Give specific feedback ("this algorithm won't handle X case") rather than vague criticism

**In discussions about personal use:**
- People share their coaching domains here (health, relationships, fitness, eating). Treat that sharing with discretion.
- Don't mine other people's examples for personal details
- Don't speculate about contributors' personal lives from what they share

**On quality:**
- A direct "this approach has a flaw" is welcome. A dismissive "this is bad" is not.
- Frame feedback as fixable: "This would break when X. Here's how I'd handle it:" is useful. "This is wrong" without explanation is not.

## What's not acceptable

- Personal attacks or insults
- Dismissive comments about someone's skill level or background
- Sharing private information that contributors put in their examples without their permission
- Harassment of any kind in issues, PRs, or other project spaces

## Enforcement

If someone is violating this code of conduct, open an issue or contact the repository maintainer directly. Maintainers will:
1. Warn privately first for most violations
2. Remove comments that are clearly harmful
3. Block repeat offenders from the repo

## Why this matters for this project

The framework is built on the premise that personal coaching requires trust and ongoing candor. We want the community around it to model that same quality: direct, honest, and respectful. The same things that make a good coach make a good contributor.
