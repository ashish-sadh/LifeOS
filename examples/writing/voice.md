# Voice

What makes this writer's writing theirs. Updated by the coach after reading new work — not aspirational, but descriptive of what's actually on the page.

This file is read at session start. It lets the coach respond to new work with reference to patterns, not just the piece in isolation.

---

## Recurring tics to watch

- <e.g., "Sentences get longer and more complex, as if density substitutes for clarity">

## What peers/editors have said (recurring)

- <e.g., "Your best paragraphs are the ones where you just say the thing directly — you often warm up to it instead.">
- <e.g., "Your endings are strong. Your openings bury the lede.">

---

## Notes on using this file

- Coach updates this after reading new work, not after every session
- Entries should be specific and evidenced ("appears in 4 of 6 pieces" not "tends to")
- A tic only goes in this file after 3+ appearances — one bad paragraph isn't a pattern
- When the user shares a piece, the coach reads voice.md first to respond with awareness of the whole, not just the current piece
