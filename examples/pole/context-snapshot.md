## Key vocabulary

- **active hang**: scapulae engaged in depression, vs. "dead hang" (passive, collapsing). Foundation movement pattern.
- **pack and rotate**: cue for shoulder mount entry — depress and posteriorly tilt scap (pack), then externally rotate the arm (rotate), THEN pull. Don't skip the rotation step on the right side.
- **scap depression**: pulling scapula down and slightly back before a pulling movement. Most important cue for invert work.
