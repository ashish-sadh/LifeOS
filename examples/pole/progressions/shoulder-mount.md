# Shoulder Mount Progression

Extracted from session notes — 7 sessions over 5 weeks. This is the first progression file for this coach; the pattern warranted extraction when shoulder mount appeared across 7 of the last 10 sessions.

## What it is

Shoulder mount: entering an inversion using the deltoid shelf rather than a bicep pull. The setup involves pressing the pole into the shoulder (meaty part of the anterior delt), then using a combination of scapular depression and lat engagement to pull the body up and over.

## Current level

**Status**: Building clean entry — 6/10 success rate on left, 3/10 on right (as of 2026-05-04).

**Baseline (2026-04-21)**: Assisted mount only. Could not achieve the position unassisted.

## Technique cues that are working

- **"Pack and rotate"**: set the shoulder (depress and posteriorly tilt the scap) BEFORE the pull. Don't pull with a passive/dangling shoulder. This cue from Sara on 2026-05-04 — clicked on attempt 9.
- **Active scap through the move**: scap depression should maintain through the full entry, not just at the start. Collapsing at the pull = scap activation dropped.
- **Lat engagement**: the pull is lat-initiated, not bicep. Think "pull your elbow to your hip" not "pull the bar to your chest."

## Cues that haven't worked

- "Squeeze the pole harder": doesn't help, creates grip fatigue
- Generic "use your shoulder": too vague, doesn't address the scap activation specifically

## Root cause of right-side failure

Right external rotation weaker than left. Result: right arm can't achieve proper setup position — pulls with bicep instead of lat+delt. Confirmed by coach Sara on 2026-05-04.

**Fix**: band pull-aparts (external rotation emphasis) before every shoulder mount session. Cue on right side: "pack and rotate" — if you skip the rotation, don't attempt the pull.

## Conditioning supporting this progression

- Banded clamshells: activating hip external rotators (hip rotation transfers to shoulder mount setup via full-body positioning)
- Band pull-aparts: direct external rotation shoulder work
- Active hang holds: training passive-to-active shoulder position (the foundational movement pattern)
- Hollow body holds: midline stability for cleaner invert position

## Sessions where this came up

- 2026-04-14: Intro to active hang (block start)
- 2026-04-21: First assisted mount attempts
- 2026-04-28: Solo conditioning (not mount work, but shoulder conditioning)
- 2026-05-04: 10 attempts each side; "pack and rotate" cue breakthrough

## 6-week milestone

**Target**: clean shoulder mount from standing start on both sides, documented on video, by end of week 6 (2026-05-26).

**Intermediate check** (end week 4): 7/10 on both sides without assist.

## What comes after

After clean standing mount: begin shoulder mount from different positions (from straddle, from climb). Long-term: use shoulder mount as entry into chopper and extended leg hang.
