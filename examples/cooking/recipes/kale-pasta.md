# Kale Pasta

**Source**: NYT Cooking, Alison Roman (https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1021389-pasta-with-garlicky-greens)
**Times tried**: 4
**Last cooked**: 2026-04-15
**Rating**: 4/5 (your version)

## Your version (current best)

**Serves**: 3-4  
**Time**: ~35 min

Ingredients:
- 1 lb rigatoni (preferred over penne — holds sauce better)
- 2 bunches Tuscan kale, stems removed, leaves torn
- 6 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 3 tbsp olive oil (down from original 5)
- 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
- 3/4 tsp kosher salt (down from original 1 tsp — your salt brand runs hot)
- 60g Pecorino Romano, grated (not Parmesan — sharper, less sweet)
- 1/2 cup pasta water (reserved)
- Juice of 1/2 lemon (your addition)

Method:
1. Salt pasta water heavily. Cook rigatoni to 2 min under al dente — it finishes in the pan.
2. Heat olive oil in wide pan on medium. Add garlic, cook 2-3 min until pale gold — not brown.
3. Add red pepper flakes, cook 30 sec.
4. Add kale in batches, tossing with tongs. Let each batch wilt 1-2 min before adding the next.
5. Reserve 1/2 cup pasta water before draining.
6. Add pasta to pan. Add 1/4 cup pasta water, toss vigorously over medium-low for 2 min.
7. Off heat: add Pecorino, lemon juice, toss again. Add more pasta water to loosen if clumping.
8. Taste and adjust salt. Serve immediately.

## Your modifications from original

- Cut olive oil from 5 tbsp to 3 — original was pooling grease
- Pecorino instead of Parmesan — Parmesan was reading sweet against the bitterness
- Lemon juice at the end — brightens without making it "lemony"
- Kale after garlic (not with): gives garlic time to soften in oil before greens crowd the pan

## What went wrong (and fixes)

- **2026-04-15**: Nearly perfect. Garlic almost burned — watch it closely, pull off heat if it's going too fast.
- **2026-02-28**: Kale wilted too fast, turned gray-green. Cause: added it all at once. Fix: in batches, let each wilt before adding more.
- **2026-01-12**: Too oily. Cause: didn't reduce olive oil from original. Fix: 3 tbsp is the right amount for this pan.
- **2025-12-01**: Dry and clumpy. Cause: forgot to reserve pasta water. Fix: set a small bowl near the pot as a reminder.

## Variations to try

- Add anchovy paste (1 tsp, with the garlic) for deeper umami
- Sub cavolo nero in winter — sturdier and slightly sweeter than Tuscan kale
- Add white beans for protein without changing the feel of the dish
- Try with pappardelle for a more substantial texture

## Coach notes

Good weeknight dish — 35 min, one pan after boiling pasta. The kale-in-batches step is the one that trips people up most. Garlic timing is the other; get distracted and you lose the dish.
