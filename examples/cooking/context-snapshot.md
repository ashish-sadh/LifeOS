## Key vocabulary

- **coconut cream separation**: open can without shaking, spoon cream into pan, heat until fat separates (shimmer at edges), THEN add paste. Frying the paste ≠ boiling it.
- **fond**: brown bits left in pan after searing. Deglaze immediately to make a pan sauce.
- **pasta water**: starchy cooking liquid; emulsifies sauce, helps it cling. Reserve before draining — can't substitute tap water.
- **blooming**: heating spices/aromatics in fat before adding liquids. The fragrance change = it worked.
- **fat calibration**: adjust recipe fat quantities downward from restaurant/cookbook levels to match home preference.
